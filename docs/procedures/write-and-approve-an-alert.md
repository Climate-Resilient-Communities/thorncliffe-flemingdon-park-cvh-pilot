# Writing and approving an alert

**Owner:** Hub Coordinator on duty (writes); the approver is a second Coordinator or Admin
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-06

Use this when the Hub learns of a disruption residents need to hear about. Aim: the acknowledgement out within 5 to 10 minutes of the first report (a team aim, not a promise). Nothing reaches residents until a second person approves it.

## Write it (Coordinator or Admin)

1. Sign in to the Hub with your authenticator code. On **Right now** (`/staff`), under **Start something**, choose:
   - **Log a disruption** (`/staff/alerts/log`) to get a short acknowledgement out first, or
   - **Compose an alert** (`/staff/alerts/compose`) when you already know enough for a full alert.
2. Answer **1. What is happening?** (one or more types), **2. Where?** (a whole neighbourhood, or buildings and floors) and **3. When did the first report reach the Hub?** (Toronto time). Press **Continue to the acknowledgement** or **Continue to the alert**.
3. On **Acknowledge the disruption** or **Write an alert**: read the suggested wording, change it if needed (**Text (English)**), set **Valid until**, and check who it is for with **Change the place** and **Change the groups**. Read the **Text message preview (English)**. Press **Save draft**.
4. Press **Submit for approval**. Wait on **Preparing your alert** while it is translated into every language (up to about a minute; you may leave the page). It ends on **Submitted for approval** with a version number.
5. If a language could not be translated, the page says so: **Try translation again** (it returns to a draft and needs a new submit), or leave it (residents in that language get the English text with "Translation not available").
6. Tell a second person it is waiting (phone or chat). You cannot approve what you wrote or changed.

## Approve it (a second Coordinator or Admin)

1. On **Right now**, under **Waiting for your approval** (longest wait first), press **Review**. The page is **Approve an alert** (`/staff/alerts/approve`).
2. Read exactly what goes out: **The text (English)**, **What goes out** (who it is for, where it goes, text message recipients and by language, estimated cost, valid until), and each language under **Other languages** (open a language to read its web text and its text message).
3. Check the notes on the page:
   - **This may duplicate another open alert**: press **Open the other alert**; if it is a duplicate, discard this one (or withdraw the other: [correcting or withdrawing](correct-or-withdraw.md)).
   - **Texts are paused; this will send when resumed**: approving still publishes; the texts wait for the resume ([pausing and resuming](pause-and-resume-texts.md)).
   - The monthly cap line ("over the monthly cap"): it only warns; approve if the alert is needed and follow [a spending cap overrun](cap-overrun.md).
4. Choose one:
   - **Approve**. If the page says the number of people changed, read the new number, tick **I have read the new number and I still want to approve.** and press **Confirm the new number and approve**.
   - **Return to author** with a note saying what to change, then **Send back with this note**. The author changes it and submits again.
   - **Discard** and **Discard it** if it must never go out. It cannot be brought back.
5. If approval is refused with "No on-call number is set": an Admin adds one on **On-call numbers** (`/staff/oncall`) first; nothing was approved.
6. On **The alert is out** (or **The acknowledgement is out**), read **What went where**. Press **See sending progress** a few minutes later to check the texts (`/staff/alerts/sending`). Texts that did not arrive: [resending texts that failed](resend-failed-texts.md).

## Keep it current

1. To add what you learn: on **Right now**, under **Running alerts**, press **Add an update** (or **Promote to full alert** for the first update to an acknowledgement). Write, **Save draft**, **Submit for approval**; a second person approves it as above.
2. A mistake in what residents read: [correcting or withdrawing an entry](correct-or-withdraw.md). When it is over: [closing an alert](close-an-alert.md).
