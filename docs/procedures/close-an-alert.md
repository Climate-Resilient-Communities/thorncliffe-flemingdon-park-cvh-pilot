# Closing an alert

**Owner:** Hub Coordinator on duty (writes the final entry); the approver is a second Coordinator or Admin
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-06

An alert closes in one of three ways. Nothing is deleted from residents' view: a closed alert moves to "alerts that have ended" with its whole thread, and nothing more can be added to it.

- **Resolved**: a final entry, approved by a second person (this page).
- **Withdrawn**: approving a withdrawal of the last entry residents can read ([correcting or withdrawing](correct-or-withdraw.md)).
- **Expired**: the expire job closes an alert whose valid-until has passed with no further update, and residents read "This alert has expired without a further update". To avoid that, update the alert or resolve it before its valid-until.

## Resolve it (Coordinator or Admin)

1. On **Right now** (`/staff`), under **Running alerts**, press **Mark resolved** on the alert. The page is **Mark resolved** (`/staff/alerts/resolve`).
2. Read the running alert above the form and **What happens when you resolve**.
3. Write the **Final entry**: what is fixed, and what to do if it is not fixed for you. Press **Save draft**, then **Submit for approval**.
4. Tell a second person it is waiting.

## Approve it (a second Coordinator or Admin)

1. On **Right now**, **Waiting for your approval**, press **Review**. The page is **Approve a final message**.
2. Read **What approving does**: it closes the alert as resolved, sends the final entry to everyone who got any entry of the alert (on the channels they got it on) and to everyone in its audience, and discards the alert's drafts and waiting entries.
3. Press **Approve**. The page ends on **The alert is resolved**.
4. On **Right now** the alert is under **Recently closed** with "Resolved". Check **See sending progress** for the final entry's texts.
