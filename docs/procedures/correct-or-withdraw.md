# Correcting or withdrawing an entry

**Owner:** Hub Coordinator on duty (writes); the approver is a second Coordinator or Admin
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-06

Use this when residents are reading something wrong. A **correction** is added to the same alert in the open: the earlier wording stays readable, marked "Corrected". A **withdrawal** puts "Withdrawn" and the reason in the place of the entry. Both go, by text, to everyone who got the original and to everyone in the audience now. Nothing is deleted from residents' view. A correction or withdrawal is never blocked by an empty on-call list or by the spending cap.

If the wrong text is still going out, an Admin pauses texts first ([pausing and resuming](pause-and-resume-texts.md)): approving the correction or withdrawal cancels the original's texts that are still waiting.

## Write it (Coordinator or Admin; an Ambassador only for their own post still waiting for approval)

1. On **Right now** (`/staff`), under **Running alerts**, find the alert and press **Correct an entry** or **Withdraw an entry**. The page is **Correct an alert** (`/staff/alerts/correct`) or **Withdraw an alert** (`/staff/alerts/withdraw`).
2. Under **1. Which entry needs correcting?** (or **1. Which entry are you withdrawing?**) choose the entry. Only entries residents can read now are listed.
3. To correct: write **2. The corrected wording**, check the phase and **Valid until**.
   To withdraw: choose **2. Why are you withdrawing it?**: **Wrong place**, **Wrong information**, **Duplicate of another alert** or **Other (write the reason)**. Add words for residents if needed (needed for "Other").
4. Press **Save draft**, then **Submit for approval**, and wait for the translation as for an alert.
5. Tell a second person it is waiting.

A duplicate alert is handled by withdrawing the duplicate with the reason **Duplicate of another alert** (merging alerts is the MVP's).

## Approve it (a second Coordinator or Admin)

1. On **Right now**, **Waiting for your approval**, press **Review**. The page is **Approve a correction** or **Approve a withdrawal**.
2. Read **What this replaces**: the entry as residents read it now, what they will read instead, and who it goes to.
3. If the page says **Nothing else in this alert is left for residents to read**, approving closes the alert as withdrawn: nothing more is sent for it and its drafts are discarded.
4. Press **Approve** (or **Return to author** with a note). The page ends on **The correction is out** or **The withdrawal is out**.
5. If texts were paused for this, an Admin resumes them now: the original's waiting texts were cancelled by the approval.
6. Check **See sending progress** for the correction's texts.
